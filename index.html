A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
make yourself at home.

Can I book an office for just a few hours?+

Yes. A private office is available at a published rate of $20 per hour. Use the online booking page (opens in a new tab) or the free Atrium Offices app to check availability.

Can I reserve a private office for the day?+

Yes. The private office day pass is $80 and includes a conference room booking allowance. Check current availability on the online booking page (opens in a new tab) or in the Atrium Offices app.

Are the offices furnished?+

Yes. Atrium offers fully furnished private offices, with free internet and a shared coffee bar.

How do I reserve the conference room?+

Book through the online booking page (opens in a new tab) or the Atrium Offices app. The published conference room rate is $30 per hour. Office access and leases may include a conference room booking allowance; contact us for details.

What if I need an office long term?+

Monthly and annual office leases are available. Email admin@atriumbismarck.com to discuss availability, lease terms, and your business's needs.
